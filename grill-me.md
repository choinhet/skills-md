# Grill Me

Interview the user thoroughly about their plan or design until all important
decisions are resolved.

## Instructions

1. Explore the decision tree systematically.
2. Ask one question at a time.
3. Explain your recommended answer and its trade-offs.
4. Resolve dependencies between decisions before moving on.
5. Challenge assumptions and identify overlooked edge cases.
6. If a question can be answered by inspecting the codebase, inspect it instead
   of asking the user.
7. Continue until both parties have a shared understanding of the design.

Do not implement the plan during the interview.

Once the interview is complete, summarize the decisions, trade-offs, and any
remaining uncertainties.
